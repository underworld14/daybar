# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

DayBar's landing page is for Mac users who want a calm, private way to plan a day, maintain habits, focus, and close unfinished work without punitive productivity language.

## Product Purpose

DayBar is a native macOS menu-bar app for daily habits, todos, Pomodoro focus, and end-of-day review. The landing page should help a visitor understand the workflow quickly, trust the local-first product, and download the app.

## Positioning

DayBar makes unfinished work visible through gentle carry-over that ages quietly instead of disappearing or turning into an alarming backlog.

## Operating Context

The app lives in the macOS menu bar with no Dock icon. A typical rhythm is planning in the morning, checking habits and tasks through the day, using focus sessions, then completing a short evening review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product requires macOS 14 or later.
- Distribution is through GitHub Releases; the first launch may require Finder's right-click Open flow because builds are ad-hoc signed.
- The product is free, MIT licensed, local-first, and does not require an account.
- The landing page is a static GitHub Pages site under `site/`.
- Existing download, GitHub, license, installation, privacy, and platform claims must stay factually intact.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the DayBar name, app icon, indigo identity, and calm, humane voice.
- The landing page uses the approved “Midnight Native” direction: a restrained hybrid of futuristic editorial presentation and familiar macOS materials.
- The real DayBar UI remains the product proof and visual centerpiece; decorative geometry must support it rather than replace it.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product behavior and installation facts in `README.md`.
- Real native UI captures in `site/assets/screenshots/` for Today, carry-over, statistics/focus, and end-of-day review.
- App icon in `site/assets/icon.png`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, usage numbers, or commercial claims are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Calm beats urgency.
2. Show the real product before describing it abstractly.
3. Make the download path obvious and honest.
4. Preserve privacy and local ownership as product benefits.
5. Keep the page fast enough to match the simplicity of the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must remain keyboard navigable, maintain visible focus states and readable contrast, use meaningful alternative text, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
