DayBar

Plan your day. Finish what you planned.

A calm macOS menu-bar app for habits, todos, and Pomodoro focus — unfinished work carries into tomorrow with a gentle nudge, not a wall of red.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

macOS 14+ · Lives in the menu bar · Free & open source

Install in a minute

  1. Download the latest DayBar-vX.Y.Z-macOS.zip from Releases.
  2. Extract and drag DayBar.app into Applications.
  3. Open once via right-click — release builds are ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper may warn on first launch. In Finder, right-click DayBar.app → Open → confirm Open. You only need to do this once.
Prefer Terminal instead?

Remove the quarantine flag, then open the app:

xattr -cr /Applications/DayBar.app
open /Applications/DayBar.app

Look for the icon in the menu bar — there is no Dock icon.

Features

Gentle carry-over

Unfinished tasks roll into tomorrow and age quietly — grey to amber, plus a calm menu-bar count. Never a wall of red.

Dayscape & focus streak

Watch your week fill with quiet ink as Pomodoros finish — a calm focus streak with one grace day, never a harsh reset.

End-of-day review

Ask “Did you finish what you planned?” — triage what’s left, jot a one-line reflection, and optionally tag your mood.

Local & private

Your plan stays on your Mac with SwiftData — no account, no cloud sync of your tasks. Optional mood suggestions use on-device Apple Intelligence when available; nothing leaves the machine.
